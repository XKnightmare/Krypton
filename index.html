01 // MISSION BRIEF

WE ONLY
RECRUIT BUILDERS.

NEXUS is the country's premier robotics and technology hackathon — a pressure-tested arena where teams are handed real, unsolved engineering constraints and expected to ship a working system before the clock hits zero.

Expect hard problems in autonomy, embedded control, computer vision, industrial automation and edge AI. No slide-deck science. Every submission must move, sense, or decide on its own. Mentors circulate, judges interrogate, and only the machines that survive testing make the stage.

COLUMN A / ELIGIBILITY

Open exclusively to undergraduate engineering and tech disciplines.

COLUMN B / ROSTER

Teams must consist of 2 to 4 active members.

COLUMN C / COMPOSITION

Multi-disciplinary or inter-departmental teams allowed.
